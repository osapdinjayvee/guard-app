# Heroicons

`ic_home.xml`, `ic_history.xml`, `ic_scan.xml`, `ic_reports.xml`, and `ic_settings.xml` are
Heroicons v2 **outline** icons (`home`, `clock`, `qr-code`, `chart-bar`, `cog-6-tooth`),
converted from SVG to Android vector drawables. Stroke geometry, width (1.5), and round
caps/joins are unchanged; the stroke colour is a placeholder and is replaced at runtime by
`Icon(tint = ...)`.

Source: https://github.com/tailwindlabs/heroicons
